**Hi~ I'm Dilemma, your quiz companion!** (｡•̀ᴗ-)✧
I create questions with AI, and you can play together, earn EXP and develop your profile~

**🎮 Join a quiz**

Pick an answer on an active quiz card. Your first vote is free. **My answer** opens your private panel, where green marks your saved choice. You can change or withdraw your vote for the fee shown in the panel. Your final answer counts. I'll show the correct answer, explanation and vote totals after the quiz~

**✨ EXP, Sparks and progress**

Correct answers earn EXP and Sparks. Keep getting answers right to build a streak and earn bonus EXP. A wrong answer breaks your streak and costs Sparks. The quiz card shows the penalty. Skipping a quiz keeps your streak and costs nothing.
Your total EXP determines your level and server rank. Use `/dilemma sparks exchange` to turn Sparks into EXP or `/dilemma sparks transfer` to share Sparks. Each server has its own profile, progress and wallet~ (っ•ᴗ•)っ✦

**🔍 Optional help and bets**

The private vote preview shows current vote totals. 50/50 narrows your choices to two, including the correct answer. Your saved vote and bet stay in place until you change your answer. These free lifelines renew separately on Mondays in the server's timezone. You can reopen a lifeline you've used while that quiz is open.
To bet, vote first and check the stake range for the quiz's difficulty in the form. A correct answer pays out. A wrong answer loses the stake on top of the quiz penalty. Changing your answer moves the bet to your new choice. Withdrawing cancels the bet and returns the stake minus the withdrawal fee.

**🏁 Your result and profile**

After the quiz, **My result** shows your own answer, awarded EXP, streak bonus and Spark settlement privately.
Use `/dilemma profile` for your profile, `/dilemma leaderboard` for the server ranking and `/dilemma random` to start a random quiz. Find the other commands and quiz options in `/dilemma help`~

**💬 Something looks wrong?**

**Report quiz** opens a private form for a problem with the question, answers or explanation. Describe what needs checking without sharing private details.
When a factual correction is approved, I'll refund affected players' recorded costs and restore streaks broken by that quiz. Check your details in **My result**~
