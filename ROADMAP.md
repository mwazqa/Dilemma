# Dilemma roadmap

Ideas agreed on October 15, 2026. Only achievements and badges are in the current scope. The remaining ideas are a backlog, not a release or deployment commitment.

## Implemented locally (Unreleased): achievements and badges

- Unlock cosmetic achievements from settled, server-specific results, including existing scores.
- Let each player choose one unlocked badge for their profile card or hide the badge.
- Keep the existing minimal navy/lavender/gold card layout and all six languages.
- Do not award bonus points or EXP for achievements.
- Delete badge selection and achievement progress with `/dilemma score forget` on that server.

## Backlog

### Weekly and monthly leaderboards

- Separate periodic rankings so new players can compete.
- Preserve the all-time leaderboard and accumulated EXP; do not reset existing scores.

### Leaderboard navigation

- Add previous/next page buttons, a "My position" action and period selection.
- Keep every query and interaction scoped to the current server.

### Statistics by topic

- Show performance per topic, strongest categories and participation-based favourite topics.
- Derive statistics from finalized quiz answers, not unconfirmed votes.

### Report incorrect questions

- Add a "Report an error" action below results.
- Let administrators review reports, invalidate incorrect quizzes and consistently correct affected scores, EXP, streaks and achievements.
- Do not automatically change results merely because a report was submitted.

### Profile customization

- Unlock avatar frames, EXP bar colours and profile titles.
- Keep the Dilemma theme and avoid overcrowding the card.
- No purchase system or monetization is part of this proposal.

## Existing longer-term idea

- Additional languages beyond English, Polish, German, Spanish, French and Japanese.
